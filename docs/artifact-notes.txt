NCM and ECM gadget modules for:
4.14.336-Nethunter-VoPi-v2-g020784b3a0ae

Target: Realme Narzo 20 / RMX2193 running the NetHunter VoPi v2 kernel.
The unified Android ROM may identify this device as RMX2020.

These are kernel modules, not a boot image or recovery installer.
Build-time checks verify every imported symbol version against the target
kernel, including module_layout. This does not prove runtime behavior.

Do not force-load these modules or use them on another kernel version.
ConfigFS binding, Android tethering integration, DHCP, Mac network traffic,
reconnect behavior and reboot persistence require separate device tests.

Source, patches and build instructions are provided in the narzo20-kernel repo.
